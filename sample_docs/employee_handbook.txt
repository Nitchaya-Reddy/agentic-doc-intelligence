Acme Labs Employee Handbook

Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per calendar year. Vacation requests longer than five consecutive business days should be submitted at least three weeks in advance.

Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Teams may designate collaboration days that require in-office attendance.

Security
Employees must use multi-factor authentication for company systems. Confidential customer information may not be copied to personal cloud storage or personal devices.

Expense Policy
Business travel expenses require itemized receipts for purchases above $25. Domestic meal expenses are reimbursed up to $70 per day.
